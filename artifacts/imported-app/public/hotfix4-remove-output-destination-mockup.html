Why it's safe: The trio was just a UI shortcut into settings.outputDestination. The dedicated NDI Output panel already gives operators a direct on/off toggle for NDI — operationally redundant with the trio. Existing operators on 'ndi' / 'both' keep auto-starting NDI on app launch via the shell effects; they just can't change it back to 'window' from this panel anymore. If they want to disable NDI, they flip the NDI Output toggle off (which is the primary control they're already using).
Net change: 1 file (settings.tsx), ~29 lines removed (the Output Destination block) + 1 help-string updated. Zero changes to store, shells, defaults, or the NDI activation pipeline. The cluttered trio disappears; everything keeps working.
